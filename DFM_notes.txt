DFM / Backdrill / Microvia policy (initial notes)

- Microvia (fanout) policy:
  * Recommended microvia drill: 0.20 mm (if vendor supports microvia), aspect ratio per fab.
  * If microvias not available, use 0.35 mm via for first breakout with short traces to inner layer.

- Backdrill:
  * DDR and PCIe via backdrill recommended for vias that traverse signal layers and impact SI.
  * Provide backdrill list: coordinates + drill depth in final manufacturing notes.

- Via annular ring:
  * Min annular ring: 0.15 mm (validate with your fabricator).

- Soldermask:
  * For BGA breakout, tent mask on thermal via if needed; coordinate with assembler.

- Test coupons:
  * Provide impedance coupons for 90Ω and 100Ω differential pairs.

Notes: refine after DRC/3D checks and SI review.
